About

I am a third-year undergraduate in electronics at Université Bourgogne Europe in Dijon, France. I spent my first two years in a combined computer-science and electronics track, then chose electronics because of digital design.

I design digital hardware, from RTL to layout, with open-source tools. My main project is RV32I-Alpha, a two-stage RISC-V (RV32I) core written in VHDL and SystemVerilog. Its instruction-level behaviour is checked with riscv-formal; it runs on an Artix-7 FPGA and is hardened to a DRC- and LVS-clean GDSII macro in the SkyWater SKY130 process.

I am also working on the measurement side of energy-efficient inference hardware: how to account for the area and energy of small neural-network accelerators end to end, including the parts that are usually left out of the comparison (input buffering, output logic, wires).

I am looking for a research internship in digital hardware design for summer 2027.
